Book a Call
This page should feel like a consultation request, not a generic contact form.
Recommended sections:
    1. Strong headline focused on business outcomes.
    2. What happens during the call (agenda and expectations).
    3. Industries and project types served.
    4. Typical engagement sizes and timelines.
    5. Client qualification questions.
    6. Embedded calendar scheduling (later).
    7. Brief questionnaire to capture business goals, current systems, timeline, budget range, and preferred contact method.
    8. Trust signals (company registration, response time, confidentiality statement).
    9. FAQs about the consultation process.

Content Style Guide
For an international executive audience:
    • Write as an experienced consultant, not a salesperson.
    • Explain complex concepts with simple analogies where they genuinely improve understanding (e.g., "A software architecture is like the blueprint of a skyscraper—changing it after construction is expensive.").
    • Support claims with reputable industry statistics and cite original sources.
    • Avoid keyword stuffing; use semantic language naturally.
    • Use short paragraphs, informative headings, diagrams where useful, and actionable takeaways.
    • Add tasteful humor sparingly to keep long-form content engaging without reducing credibility.
