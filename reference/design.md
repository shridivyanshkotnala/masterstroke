# Trust Architecture

Every page should accumulate credibility through multiple, distributed signals rather than a single testimonials section.

**Trust layers include:**

* Company registration and legal identity.
* Founder profile and technical leadership.
* Engineering methodology and development standards.
* Case studies with measurable business outcomes.
* Technology expertise and certifications (where applicable).
* Security, privacy, and compliance commitments.
* Transparent engagement process.
* FAQs that reduce buyer uncertainty.

---

# Design Tokens

All visual decisions should be centralized as design tokens to ensure consistency.

**Core token categories:**

* Colors
* Typography
* Spacing
* Border radius
* Shadows
* Breakpoints
* Z-index
* Motion durations
* Easing curves
* Icon sizing
* Grid and container widths

---

# Final Design Vision

If someone lands on the site for **five seconds**, they should think:

> "This is a serious engineering consultancy."

After **two minutes**:

> "These people understand technology and business."

After **ten minutes**:

> "They're likely capable of leading our next software initiative."

That is a higher-value positioning than trying to look like another modern agency. The design should support the content, the content should demonstrate expertise, and every interaction should reduce friction toward booking a strategy call.
