# 18. Initial Launch Plan

The first release should prioritize depth over breadth while establishing topical authority.

| Section                              |   Pages |
| ------------------------------------ | ------: |
| Homepage                             |       1 |
| Company                              |       8 |
| Core Services                        |      18 |
| Industry Pages                       |      12 |
| Solution Pages                       |      10 |
| Technologies                         |      15 |
| Case Studies                         |      10 |
| Resources (guides, checklists, etc.) |       8 |
| Blog Articles                        |      50 |
| Legal & Trust Pages                  |       8 |
| **Total Initial Pages**              | **140** |

With approximately **140 high-quality, interlinked pages** supported by strong technical SEO, structured data, and comprehensive topical clusters, Kotnala Consultancy can launch with enough topical depth to compete for meaningful international search visibility rather than starting with a thin brochure website and trying to scale later. This approach positions the site as an authoritative resource rather than just another software agency homepage.
